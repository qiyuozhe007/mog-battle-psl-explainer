What is PSL in mog battles?

In mog battles, PSL is a looks-rating scale used to score each side; in Omoggle Game, a free browser 1v1 mog battle, that PSL score is computed on-device and combined with audience votes and an ELO rating.

"PSL" comes from online looksmaxxing communities, where it is used as a shorthand rating scale. A "mog battle" is a head-to-head comparison where two people are rated against each other. Turning that into a game means adding a score, a decision and a ranking.

How PSL fits into a game round

  1. Two players enter a 1v1 round.
  2. A PSL-style score is generated on the player's device in the browser.
  3. The audience votes on who wins.
  4. The result updates each player's ELO.

If you want to try the format rather than read about it, the free mog battle game at omogglegame.com runs in the browser.

Short Q&A

Is the PSL score the final verdict? No, audience votes decide the round.

Why ELO? ELO adjusts your rating based on the strength of who you beat or lose to.

Is it affiliated with omoggle.com? No, omogglegame.com is an independent site.
